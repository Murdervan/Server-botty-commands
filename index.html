Casino & Economy

Coins, bets, drops and leaderboard

ECONOMY
!lotto 10

Buy lottery tickets. Choose an amount — up to 100 tickets.

!lottokupon

View your active lottery tickets.

!lottostats

View lottery statistics and previous winners.

!bal @user

Check your balance or another user’s balance.

!rob @user

Try to steal coins from another user once per day. Success chance is about 50%; if you fail, you lose coins instead.

!drop

Get random drops (for members with the Casino role). Maximum 3 per time.

!guessdrop 1-25

Guess a number. A correct guess awards 2,500–10,000 coins. Maximum 3 times per day.

!scoreboard

See who is leading the scoreboard.

!blackjack 100

Play blackjack with a bet. React with 👊 Hit, ✋ Stand, 💰 Double Down, or ✌ Split.

!roulette 50 red

Play roulette. Choose red, black, green, or a number. You can also enter the bet and color in reverse order.

!coinflip 100

Flip a coin with a bet. Choose using the red or blue reaction.
